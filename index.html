SRC SOURCES & REFERENCES

All content in this training hub is based on fictional scenarios using notional countries developed by US Army TRADOC for educational purposes. No classified, CUI, or FOUO material has been used. All sources listed below are publicly available and unclassified.

Scenario Framework
TRADOC ODIN — Decisive Action Training Environment (DATE)
US Army Training and Doctrine Command — Operational Environment and Threat Analysis Directorate. Provides the fictional nation-state construct (Donovia, Gorgas, Atropia, Limaria, Ariana) used across all scenario trainers.
odin.tradoc.army.mil
TRADOC Pamphlet 525-3-1 — The U.S. Army in Multi-Domain Operations 2028
Headquarters, US Army Training and Doctrine Command (Dec 2018). Unclassified concept document establishing the Multi-Domain Operations (MDO) framework referenced in scenario design.
Intelligence Doctrine
JP 2-0 — Joint Intelligence
Joint Chiefs of Staff (01 Oct 2013). Defines the joint intelligence enterprise, collection disciplines (GEOINT, SIGINT, HUMINT, MASINT, OSINT), all-source analysis, and the PIR/IR process.
FM 2-0 — Intelligence
Headquarters, Department of the Army (06 Jul 2018). Army intelligence doctrine covering collection management and ISR operations.
ADP 2-0 — Intelligence
Headquarters, Department of the Army (Jul 2019). Foundational Army doctrine defining intelligence principles and the commander's critical information requirements (CCIR) process.
ATP 2-01 — Planning Requirements and Assessing Collection
Headquarters, Department of the Army. ISR tasking and collection management procedures.
ICD 203 — Analytic Standards
Office of the Director of National Intelligence (Jan 2015). Unclassified directive establishing analytic tradecraft standards including sourcing, confidence levels, and BLUF formatting.
Air Force Doctrine
AFDP 2-0 — Global Integrated ISR Operations
Air Force Doctrine Publication. Covers Air Force ISR concepts, collection management, and the integration of ISR capabilities into joint operations.
doctrine.af.mil
AFDP 3-0 — Operations and Planning
Air Force Doctrine Publication. Foundational Air Force operations and planning doctrine used in air operations scenario design.
AFDP 3-52 — Airspace Control
Air Force Doctrine Publication. Joint airspace control doctrine including the Airspace Control Order (ACO) and coordination with ground forces.
AFDP 3-60 — Targeting
Air Force Doctrine Publication. Joint targeting cycle (D3A/F3EAD), target development, and effects assessment referenced in air operations phases.
Cyberspace Operations
JP 3-12 — Cyberspace Operations
Joint Chiefs of Staff (08 Jun 2018). Joint doctrine for offensive (OCO) and defensive (DCO) cyberspace operations and the cyber threat landscape.
Cyber Kill Chain® Framework
Hutchins, Cloppert & Amin (2011). Intelligence-Driven Computer Network Defense — Lockheed Martin. Publicly available white paper used to structure cyber inject analysis tasks.
MITRE ATT&CK® Framework
MITRE Corporation. Publicly available knowledge base of adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) used to categorize threat actor behavior.
attack.mitre.org
Electromagnetic Spectrum & Electronic Warfare
JP 3-85 — Joint Electromagnetic Spectrum Operations
Joint Chiefs of Staff (22 May 2020). Defines JEMSO, electronic warfare (EW), electronic attack/protection/support, and spectrum management.
FM 3-12 — Cyberspace and Electronic Warfare Operations
Headquarters, Department of the Army (11 Apr 2017). Army doctrine for integrated cyberspace and EW operations including GNSS jamming and counter-UAS.
Geospatial Intelligence
GEOINT Basic Doctrine Publication 1-0 (BDP 1-0)
National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA). Unclassified foundational doctrine covering imagery intelligence (IMINT), FMV, wide-area motion imagery (WAMI), and synthetic aperture radar (SAR).
Information Operations
JP 3-13 — Information Operations
Joint Chiefs of Staff (20 Nov 2014). Defines IO core capabilities including military deception (MILDEC), MISO, OPSEC, and the information environment.
FM 3-53 — Military Information Support Operations
Headquarters, Department of the Army (03 Jan 2013). MISO planning and execution doctrine informing disinformation and social media narrative analysis tasks.
Space Operations
JP 3-14 — Space Operations
Joint Chiefs of Staff (10 Apr 2018, Change 1 — 26 Oct 2020). Covers space control, ISR from space, satellite communications, PNT, and space situational awareness.
